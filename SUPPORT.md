# Support

- **Questions and bugs:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/raldblox/flying-money/issues).
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue; use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/raldblox/flying-money/security/advisories/new). See [SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).
- **Start here:** [docs](https://useflyingmoney.vercel.app/docs), [FAQ](https://useflyingmoney.vercel.app/docs/faq), and `npx -y @flying-money/mcp onboard` for a new agent.
- **Lost keys:** the project never holds keys or money and cannot recover them. Budgets return to their funder after the end date.

Flying Money is a beta and is not audited. Mainnets hold real money under caps fixed in the contract.
